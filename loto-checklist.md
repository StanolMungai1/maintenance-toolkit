# Lockout / Tagout (LOTO) Checklist

A general checklist for isolating machinery before maintenance. **Always follow your site's written, machine-specific LOTO procedure and the safety rules that apply where you work.** This sheet is a guide, not a replacement for training or the official procedure.

**Golden rules:** one person, one lock. Only the person who fitted a lock removes it. Never rely on a switch or a stop button alone.

---

## Before you start

- [ ] I am trained and authorised to carry out LOTO on this equipment
- [ ] I have the machine-specific procedure and know all its energy sources
- [ ] I have my personal lock, tag, and the correct PPE
- [ ] The job is planned and the work permit (if required) is in place

## Isolation steps

- [ ] **1. Identify** every energy source: electrical, mechanical, hydraulic, pneumatic, thermal, chemical, gravity and stored energy
- [ ] **2. Notify** operators and anyone affected that the machine will be shut down and locked out
- [ ] **3. Shut down** the machine using the normal stop procedure
- [ ] **4. Isolate** each energy source (switch off, close valves, disconnect supply)
- [ ] **5. Lock and tag** each isolation point with your own lock and a tag showing your name, date and reason
- [ ] **6. Release stored energy**: bleed pressure, block or lower raised parts, discharge capacitors, let hot parts cool, stop moving parts
- [ ] **7. Verify zero energy**: try to start the machine from the normal controls, then test for dead with a suitable tester. Return the controls to off afterwards
- [ ] **8. Start work** only after every step above is confirmed

## Returning to service

- [ ] Tools, spare parts and waste removed from the machine
- [ ] Guards, covers and safety devices refitted and working
- [ ] Everyone is clear of the machine
- [ ] Each person removes **their own** lock and tag
- [ ] Operators and affected staff told that the machine is about to be re-energised
- [ ] Re-energise, test run, and check for correct operation
- [ ] Work and LOTO recorded in the log below
